Hello App Review,

Photo Mirror Sync mirrors the user's photo library to their own self-hosted Immich photo server (open source, https://immich.app). It signs in to that server with the user's email and password; there is no account with us and no other backend.

Demo server for review:
  Server: <DEMO SERVER URL>
  Email: <DEMO EMAIL>
  Password: <DEMO PASSWORD>

How to test:
1. Enter the server URL, email and password above and tap "Sign In".
2. Tap "Allow Access" and choose "Allow Full Access" (full access is required: with limited access, unselected photos would look deleted and would be removed from the server).
3. "Run Dry Run" (on by default) only reports what would happen and changes nothing.
4. To really sync: Settings > turn off "Dry run", optionally set "Upload limit per run" to 25 photos, then tap "Sync Now". Uploaded photos appear in the demo server's web interface and under "Mirrored photos" > "Recently Mirrored".
5. Background: on iOS 26 the sync continues as a continued processing task after leaving the app, with progress in the system UI. A notification summarizes the result.

Deleting a photo in the Photos app moves it to the server's trash on the next sync (never permanently deleted); more than 5% of the library at once needs confirmation.
